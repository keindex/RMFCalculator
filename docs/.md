# FSU模型增加\(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)（isovector\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)scalar，同位旋标量\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)标量）介子
> $\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}$介子：**同位旋矢量\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)标量介子**，ISO\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)scalar\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)isovector，三重态$\delta^{(0,\pm)}$，场$\vec{\delta}$，质量$m_\delta\approx980\,\text{MeV}$，只作用于同位旋不对称项，只改变标量同位旋矢量密度，不改变对称核物质饱和点性质。
>
> 注意区分：$\Delta(1232)$是重子共振；$\boldsymbol{\delta}$是介子，下面是**\(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)介子**。

## 完整拉氏密度（FSUGold基础 + \(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)介子，保留原有$\zeta,\Lambda_v$）
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L} &= \bar{\psi}\left(i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu-M\right)\psi
+ g_\sigma\sigma\bar\psi\psi
+ g_\delta\vec{\delta}\cdot\bar\psi\vec\tau\psi
-g_\omega\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\omega_\mu\psi
-\frac{g_\rho}{2}\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\vec\tau\cdot\vec\rho_\mu\psi \\
&+\frac12\left(\partial^\mu\sigma\partial_\mu\sigma-m_\sigma^2\sigma^2\right)
-\frac{\kappa}{3!}(g_\sigma\sigma)^3-\frac{\lambda}{4!}(g_\sigma\sigma)^4 \\
&+\boldsymbol{\frac12\left(\partial^\mu\vec\delta\cdot\partial_\mu\vec\delta - m_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta\right)} \\
&-\frac14\Omega^{\mu\nu}\Omega_{\mu\nu}+\frac12m_\omega^2\omega^\mu\omega_\mu
+\frac{\zeta}{4!}\big(g_\omega^2\omega^\mu\omega_\mu\big)^2 \\
&-\frac14\vec R^{\mu\nu}\cdot\vec R_{\mu\nu}+\frac12m_\rho^2\vec\rho^\mu\cdot\vec\rho_\mu \\
&+\Lambda_v\big(g_\rho^2\vec\rho^\mu\vec\rho_\mu\big)\big(g_\omega^2\omega^\mu\omega_\mu\big) \\
&-\frac14F^{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}-\frac e2\bar\psi\gamma^\mu(1+\tau_3)A_\mu\psi
\end{aligned}
$$
新增项：
1. 重子\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)\(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)耦合：$\boldsymbol{g_\delta\vec{\delta}\cdot\bar\psi\vec\tau\psi}$
2. \(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)介子动能+质量项：$\boldsymbol{\dfrac12\left(\partial^\mu\vec\delta\cdot\partial_\mu\vec\delta - m_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta\right)}$

> 很多FSU\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)\(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)版本还会加入**\(\sigma\)\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)\(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)交叉自耦合 $\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,\sigma^2\,\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta$**，放在介子势部分：
$$
\mathcal L_{\text{meson\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)
$$
该交叉项是FSU\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)\(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)6.2 / FSU\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)\(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)6.7模型的关键，用来调节\(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)场的介质演化，原始FSUGold没有这一项。

场强不变：
$\Omega_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\omega_\nu-\partial_\nu\omega_\mu,\ \vec R_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\vec\rho_\nu-\partial_\nu\vec\rho_\mu$。

## RMF平均场近似下场方程（静态，$\partial_\mu=0$）
对称物质：$\sigma=\bar\sigma,\omega_0=\bar\omega$；不对称物质额外$\rho_0^{(3)}=\bar\rho,\delta^{(3)}=\bar\delta$
$$
\begin{aligned}
m_\sigma^2\bar\sigma+\kappa g_\sigma^3\bar\sigma^2+\lambda g_\sigma^4\bar\sigma^3
+2\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}g_\sigma^2 g_\delta^2\bar\sigma\bar\delta^2 &= g_\sigma\rho_s \\
m_\delta^2\bar\delta+2\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}g_\sigma^2 g_\delta^2\bar\sigma^2\bar\delta &= g_\delta\left(\rho_{s,p}-\rho_{s,n}\right) \\
m_\omega^2\bar\omega+\frac{\zeta}{6}g_\omega^4\bar\omega^3+2\Lambda_v g_\omega^2 g_\rho^2\bar\omega\bar\rho^2 &= g_\omega\rho_B \\
m_\rho^2\bar\rho+2\Lambda_v g_\omega^2 g_\rho^2\bar\omega^2\bar\rho &= \frac{g_\rho}{2}\left(\rho_p-\rho_n\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
$\rho_s=\rho_{s,p}+\rho_{s,n}$总标量密度；$\rho_B=\rho_p+\rho_n$重子数密度。

> 若$\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}=0$，则\(\boldsymbol{\vec{\delta}}\)场方程简化：
$$
m_\delta^2\bar\delta = g_\delta\left(\rho_{s,p}-\rho_{s,n}\right)
$$

## 区分：如果你本意是加$\boldsymbol{\Delta(1232)}$重子共振（自旋3/2 Rarita\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)Schwinger场）
$\psi^\nu_\Delta$是Rarita\(\mathcal L_{\text{meson‑pot}} \supset -\Lambda_{\sigma\delta}\,(g_\sigma\sigma)^2\,(g_\delta^2\vec\delta\cdot\vec\delta)\)Schwinger旋量场，拉氏量增加Δ部分：
$$
\mathcal L_\Delta=\sum_{\Delta}\bar\psi^\nu_\Delta\left(i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu-M_\Delta-g_\sigma^\Delta\sigma
-g_\delta^\Delta\vec\delta\cdot\vec\tau
-g_\omega^\Delta\gamma^\mu\omega_\mu
-\frac12g_\rho^\Delta\gamma^\mu\vec\tau\cdot\vec\rho_\mu-M_\Delta\right)\psi_{\Delta\nu}
$$
$M_\Delta=1232\,\text{MeV}$；常用比例$x_{m\Delta}=g_{m\Delta}/g_{mN}$，$x_{\sigma\Delta}\approx1.0\sim1.3,\ x_{\omega\Delta}\approx1.0\sim1.1$，$x_{\rho\Delta}\approx1.0$，没有普适确定参数，需要拟合中子星物态方程。
